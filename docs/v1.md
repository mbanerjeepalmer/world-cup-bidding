# v1

We are building a football World Cup bidding game.

Here's how it works:
- Each person bids on a team.
  - The bidding increments are standard.
- Teams win points based on their result in the tournament.
  - If a team comes fourth in its group they get zero points. If they come third they get one. If they win but get knocked out immediately they get three points. If they win the overall competition there is some overall number of points they win that makes sense.
- The ratio between the team's points won and the bid amount is the end score.
  - So if you overpay for Brazil and they win the cup, you won't win the game.
  - If you pick up Paraguay, Curaçao and Saudi Arabia for nothing and all do well in the group, perhaps you win overall.
- The auction closes one hour before kickoff of the first match.
- The imaginary currency should be BonBons.
